IMPORTANT — PLEASE READ BEFORE BOOKING

AGE

Sessions are for adults only.

I do not provide sessions to minors or anyone under the legal age.

INFIDELITY

I do not encourage or support marital or sexual infidelity in any form.

Calling a relationship "spiritual" does not change this principle.

If you are married or in a relationship and are entering a relationship with someone else under any label, do not book a session with me to justify this relationship or get encouragement from me.

A relationship is a bond built on challenges, not on infidelity.

Whatever your story, this is a clear principle for me and I hope it is respected.

IF YOU ARE ALREADY CERTAIN

If you are already completely certain about your position or about your interpretation of the relationship, please do not book a diagnostic session with me.

Do not book the session just to get an opinion that confirms an idea you have already decided.

I do not provide sessions to confirm illusions, stories, or conclusions that have been decided in advance.

SEXUAL RELATIONSHIPS

If there is a sexual relationship between you and the person concerned, I do not offer a diagnostic session for that relationship.

My position on this point is clear, so do not book in order to try to change it or convince me otherwise.

This is not because of religion at all — it is because of its course and its meaning.

DREAMS

Dream interpretation is NOT part of my sessions.

SESSION SUBJECT

The session is dedicated to the subject that was agreed upon.

I do not want the specified time turned into side conversations or topics unrelated to the session subject.

SESSION TIME

Session duration is one hour.

When the agreed time ends, the session ends. The session is not automatically extended.

PROVIDE RELEVANT DETAILS

I want you to provide the important details I need clearly from the start.

Do not wait until the diagnostic session is over to remember fundamental details or change the scenario by adding essential information that was not mentioned earlier.

Hiding essential information and adding it after the analysis is complete can change the data, and therefore I do not consider this a professional way of working.

OTHER GUIDES / OTHER SESSIONS

If you are currently working with another mentor, guide, or person on the same subject, I prefer that you do not mix multiple sessions and interpretations at the same time.

Mixing multiple interpretations and opinions at the same time can confuse your thoughts and affect the way you present information, and may make the session less clear and the analysis more difficult.

This is a professional boundary, not a statement that another practitioner is necessarily wrong.

RESPECT IS REQUIRED

I do not accept insults, profanity, deliberate provocation, threats, or starting a fight.

You have the right to disagree with me, but you do not have the right to cross the boundaries of respect.

If disrespect continues, the session may be ended.

Private sessions are exploratory services and are not medical, psychiatric, psychological, or emergency care. No medical or psychiatric diagnosis is provided.
